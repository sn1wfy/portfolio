Lunar textures in this folder are derived from public-domain NASA / USGS data.

moon-albedo-4k.jpg, moon-albedo-lo.jpg
  Source: Clementine UVVIS 750 nm Global Mosaic 118 m v2.1
  (NASA / DoD Clementine mission; USGS Astrogeology Science Center),
  via https://planetarymaps.usgs.gov/mosaic/
  Processing: area-downsampled to 4096x2048 equirectangular (lon 0 at centre), data gaps
  filled, N-S orbit-track striping removed, polar caps softened, encoded as sRGB greyscale.

moon-relief-2k.jpg   (R,G = tangent-space normal X/Y [east, north]; B = 8-bit height)
  Source: LRO LOLA LDEM_16 gridded topography (NASA Goddard Space Flight Center, LOLA team),
  via the NASA PDS Geosciences Node, https://pds-geosciences.wustl.edu/
  Height encoding: B/255 maps linearly to -9200 m .. +10900 m relative to 1737.4 km.
  Normals computed on the sphere with 3x vertical exaggeration.

The LOLA LDEM is the same elevation product used by NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio
"CGI Moon Kit" (https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720); the SVS server was unreachable at build time,
so the albedo comes from the USGS-hosted Clementine mosaic instead of the SVS LROC colour map.
